Help & FAQ

How FocusPaw tracks your focus, protects your privacy, and helps you build better browsing habits.

Your Privacy Matters

100% Local Storage

All your browsing data is stored locally on your device using Chrome's built-in storage. Nothing leaves your computer.

No External Servers

FocusPaw never connects to external servers. There are no analytics, no tracking, and no cloud sync.

No Account Required

You don't need to sign up or create an account. Just install and start tracking your focus.

You Control Your Data

Export your data anytime in JSON or CSV format, or delete everything with one click in Settings.

Key Concepts

Streak

A streak counts consecutive days where you stayed within ALL your set limits.

How it works:

  • Set daily limits on distracting sites (e.g., Twitter: 10 visits, Reddit: 5 visits)
  • Each day you stay under ALL limits counts as a streak day
  • Exceed any limit? Your streak resets to 0
  • The longer your streak, the better your focus habits!
Start with generous limits and gradually tighten them as your streak grows.

Focus Score

Your Focus Score is a daily rating from 0-100 that measures how well you're managing your browsing habits.

Score Components:

Limits Compliance 40%

How well you stayed within your set limits today

Visit Reduction 30%

Whether you're reducing total visits compared to last week

Streak Bonus 20%

Longer streaks = higher score contribution

Domain Focus 10%

Fewer unique sites visited = better focus

A score of 70+ means you're doing great! Aim for consistency over perfection.

Frequently Asked Questions

FocusPaw tracks how many times you switch to each website (domain). It counts each time you navigate to or activate a tab with a different domain. It does NOT track:

  • What you do on each site
  • Time spent on pages
  • Page content or your inputs
  • Your browsing in Incognito mode

You can set two types of limits for any domain:

  • Daily Limit: Maximum visits allowed per day (resets at midnight)
  • 5-Hour Window: Maximum visits within any 5-hour period

When you exceed a limit, you'll see a friendly block page encouraging you to get back to work. Limits help you build awareness of your browsing patterns.

  • JSON: Machine-readable format, great for backups or custom analysis
  • CSV: Spreadsheet-compatible, opens in Excel or Google Sheets

You have full control over your data:

  • Delete specific domain: Right-click a domain in the table and select "Delete"
  • Delete everything: Go to Settings → Data Management → "Reset All Focus Data"

Deleted data cannot be recovered, so export first if you want a backup!

Daily stats reset at midnight in your local timezone. Your streak is evaluated at the end of each day—if you stayed under all limits, your streak continues!

FocusPaw doesn't track certain pages:

  • Chrome internal pages (chrome://, chrome-extension://)
  • New Tab pages
  • Incognito/Private browsing
  • Local files (file://)

This is by design to focus on actual web browsing habits.
